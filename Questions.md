### 📝 SITE VISIT QUESTIONNAIRE: HYDERABAD RESIDENTIAL PROJECTS

#### 🛡️ Part 1: Verification, Legal, & Trust-Building Questions
Use these to check the builder's transparency, legal health, and HYDRAA compliance.

1. "Can I see a copy of the Irrigation Department NOC and the Final GHMC Sanctioned Plan?"
   • Why: Directly confirms HYDRAA compliance and ensures the physical layout matches what the city officially approved.

2. "Is this a Joint Venture (JV) or outright owned land? If a JV, has the Landowner Allocation Share been registered?"
   • Why: Prevents you from buying a unit tied up in developer-landowner allocation disputes.

3. "Are there any active legal disputes, litigations, or encumbrances on this specific survey number?"
   • Why: Forces the sales team to explicitly state the status of the land title on the record.

4. "What construction technology is being used for the internal partition walls?"
   • Why: Confirms if they are using solid monolithic concrete casting throughout, or shifting to cheaper hollow/lightweight blocks inside the flats (which reduces soundproofing).

---

#### ⏳ Part 2: Timeline & "Move-In ASAP" Questions
Use these to gauge the true completion speed and hold the builder accountable to deadlines.

5. "What is the exact target date for applying for the Occupancy Certificate (OC) for this specific tower?"
   • Why: You cannot legally occupy the flat, register it, or get permanent utility connections without an OC. 

6. "What are the physical construction milestones for my next 3 demand notes (payments)?"
   • Why: Helps you align your bank loan disbursements or personal funds with their actual building velocity.

7. "What is the specific compensation penalty clause in the Agreement of Sale if delivery extends past the promised date?"
   • Why: Proves if the builder legally backs their timeline commitments in alignment with TS-RERA guidelines.

8. "Will the clubhouse, swimming pool, and main approach road be handed over simultaneously with the tower possession?"
   • Why: Builders frequently deliver flats while delaying social amenities and common infrastructure for an extra 12 to 18 months.

---

#### 💡 Pro-Tip Checklist For the Visit:
• Do not just look at the premium show flat. Insist on walking into the actual under-construction tower.
• Check the exact physical distance (setback) between the boundary wall and the building.
• Look at the proximity of high-tension power lines, active open drains, or upcoming commercial zones nearby.

### 🏢 SPECIFIC RED-FLAG QUESTIONS FOR INDIS SIA PROSPERA

• Context: Sia Prospera is a Joint Venture (JV) between INDIS and Sia Constructions. The physical building structures are already fully standing (structural casting hit 100% completion), and active internal works—like first-coat internal painting and terrace waterproofing—are underway. However, official possession is set for June 2028. 

• Ask the Sales Executive:
  1. "Since the main structural skeleton of the towers is already 100% complete, what is the exact timeline for the completion of the secondary civil structures? Specifically, what is the target date for finishing the main gate fabrication, entrance civil works, and the east-side boundary wall?"
  
  2. "Given that the structures are standing but the official RERA handover date is June 2028, why is there a multi-year gap left for finishing? Can you give me a written breakdown of the internal finishing, plumbing, and electrical milestones scheduled between now and 2028?"
  
  3. "Since this is a Joint Venture with Sia Constructions, can I see the Joint Development Agreement (JDA) to verify exactly which flat numbers belong to INDIS and which belong to the landowner/Sia? If I buy this unit, will the sale deed be signed by INDIS or Sia?"
  
  4. "What is the status of the external infrastructure? When will the west-face podium waterproofing be completed, and when will the G+5 clubhouse civil construction switch from structure to final interior handover?"

• Watch out for: Short-shifting blame between the two JV partners (INDIS vs. Sia). Since the concrete structure is done, your risk isn't the building collapsing or halting halfway—it is "rolling delays" on the finishing touches, electrical substations, and permanent water connections. Ensure their answers align strictly with their official RERA timeline (RERA ID: P02400007079).


### 🏢 SPECIFIC RED-FLAG QUESTIONS FOR OTHER SITES

#### 1. At Team4 Nyla (Miyapur, NH 65)
• Context: It features a massive vertical stretch (35 floors) hosting 946 units over ~6 acres, with a RERA deadline of October 2027.
• Ask the Sales Executive: 
  "Given the 35-floor height and high density across 3 towers, what is the exact layout of the elevator banks? How many high-speed elevators are assigned per tower, and what is the calculated wait-time capacity during morning peak hours?"
• Watch out for: Vague answers like "plenty of lifts." Look at the concrete shell to see if lift cores match the promised density. Also, check how close the towers sit to the main highway noise.

#### 2. At Candeur 40 (Miyapur Main Road)
• Context: This is an ultra-tall 40-floor sky-rise reaching its final handover phase (RERA target September 2026). It is very dense, with nearly 960+ units.
• Ask the Sales Executive: 
  "Since the towers are nearing final structural completion, can I see the active status of the municipal permanent water grid line connection and the fire safety clearance certificates for the upper floors?"
• Watch out for: Relying heavily on water tankers post-move-in. Since it is highly advanced in construction, demand to see a physically completed sample of the common lobby and layout finishes rather than just the model flat.

#### 3. At KSR Greens (Madeenaguda / Hafeezpet)
• Context: KSR is scaling up from lower-density projects to a massive 30+ floor structure targeting mid-2026.
• Ask the Sales Executive: 
  "Since this is a major vertical scale-up for KSR compared to standalone mid-rises, who is the structural engineering consultant or Project Management Consultant (PMC) auditing the high-rise engineering quality, and can I see their structural stability report?"
• Watch out for: Localized masonry or finishing delays. Walk around the boundaries to check the exact setback distance separating the high-rise structure from neighboring properties.
